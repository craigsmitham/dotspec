# ISO/IEC 25010:2023 starter

## Description

A dotspec starter for reviewing the nine product-quality characteristics and all 40 sub-characteristics in ISO/IEC 25010:2023. These short explanations are original authoring prompts, not standard definitions or a conformance checklist. Consult the standard for authoritative definitions and guidance.

## Edition

2023

## Sources

[ISO/IEC 25010:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html)

## Quality attributes

### Functional suitability

**ID:** functional-suitability

Consider whether the supplied functions address the intended tasks and produce the needed results.

### Functional completeness

**ID:** functional-completeness
**Sub-attribute of:** functional-suitability

Which intended tasks still lack a supplied function?

### Functional correctness

**ID:** functional-correctness
**Sub-attribute of:** functional-suitability

What results must each function produce, and how will their correctness be checked?

### Functional appropriateness

**ID:** functional-appropriateness
**Sub-attribute of:** functional-suitability

How directly do the supplied functions help people complete their intended tasks?

### Performance efficiency

**ID:** performance-efficiency

Consider response times, workload capacity, and resources consumed during operation.

### Time behaviour

**ID:** time-behaviour
**Sub-attribute of:** performance-efficiency

What response times and processing rates are needed for representative workloads?

### Resource utilization

**ID:** resource-utilization
**Sub-attribute of:** performance-efficiency

What budgets apply to processor time, memory, storage, network traffic, and other resources?

### Capacity

**ID:** capacity
**Sub-attribute of:** performance-efficiency

What workload and data limits must the product support while meeting its other requirements?

### Compatibility

**ID:** compatibility

Consider how the product exchanges information and shares its environment with other products.

### Co-existence

**ID:** co-existence
**Sub-attribute of:** compatibility

What other products share the environment, and how will interference be limited?

### Interoperability

**ID:** interoperability
**Sub-attribute of:** compatibility

What information must be exchanged with other systems and correctly used afterward?

### Interaction capability

**ID:** interaction-capability

Consider how people learn, understand, and use the product to accomplish their tasks.

### Appropriateness recognizability

**ID:** appropriateness-recognizability
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

How will prospective users recognize whether the product fits their tasks?

### Learnability

**ID:** learnability
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

What must new users learn, and how much time and assistance should that require?

### Operability

**ID:** operability
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

How easily can users control the product and carry out routine work?

### User error protection

**ID:** user-error-protection
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

Which foreseeable user mistakes should be prevented or caught before they cause damage?

### User engagement

**ID:** user-engagement
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

What aspects of the interaction encourage people to continue using the product?

### Inclusivity

**ID:** inclusivity
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

Which differences in language, background, age, or ability must the interaction accommodate?

### User assistance

**ID:** user-assistance
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

What help should users receive when they need guidance or have difficulty completing a task?

### Self-descriptiveness

**ID:** self-descriptiveness
**Sub-attribute of:** interaction-capability

How will the interface make available actions, current state, and next steps understandable?

### Reliability

**ID:** reliability

Consider continuity of service, failures, and recovery under the intended operating conditions.

### Faultlessness

**ID:** faultlessness
**Sub-attribute of:** reliability

Which failures during ordinary operation are unacceptable, and how will their frequency be assessed?

### Availability

**ID:** availability
**Sub-attribute of:** reliability

When must the product be ready for use, and what interruption limits apply?

### Fault tolerance

**ID:** fault-tolerance
**Sub-attribute of:** reliability

Which component or environmental faults must the product withstand while continuing its required work?

### Recoverability

**ID:** recoverability
**Sub-attribute of:** reliability

Following an interruption, what service and data must be restored, and within what limits?

### Security

**ID:** security

Consider protection of information and operations against unauthorized access or interference.

### Confidentiality

**ID:** confidentiality
**Sub-attribute of:** security

Which information must be restricted, and who is permitted to read it?

### Integrity

**ID:** integrity
**Sub-attribute of:** security

Which data and operations must be protected against unauthorized changes?

### Non-repudiation

**ID:** non-repudiation
**Sub-attribute of:** security

What evidence must remain available to establish that an action or exchange occurred?

### Accountability

**ID:** accountability
**Sub-attribute of:** security

Which actions must be traceable to the responsible person or system?

### Authenticity

**ID:** authenticity
**Sub-attribute of:** security

How will claimed identities and the origins of information be verified?

### Resistance

**ID:** resistance
**Sub-attribute of:** security

Which attacks must the product resist while protecting its intended operation?

### Maintainability

**ID:** maintainability

Consider the effort and confidence involved in understanding, testing, and changing the product.

### Modularity

**ID:** modularity
**Sub-attribute of:** maintainability

How should responsibilities be separated so a change has a bounded effect?

### Reusability

**ID:** reusability
**Sub-attribute of:** maintainability

Which assets should be usable in other products or contexts, and with what adaptation?

### Analysability

**ID:** analysability
**Sub-attribute of:** maintainability

How will maintainers diagnose problems and estimate the consequences of proposed changes?

### Modifiability

**ID:** modifiability
**Sub-attribute of:** maintainability

What kinds of change must be practical without introducing unacceptable defects or effort?

### Testability

**ID:** testability
**Sub-attribute of:** maintainability

How will the product expose the controls and observations needed to verify its behavior?

### Flexibility

**ID:** flexibility

Consider adjustment to different environments, capacities, and future needs.

### Adaptability

**ID:** adaptability
**Sub-attribute of:** flexibility

Which operating environments and usage contexts must the product accommodate?

### Scalability

**ID:** scalability
**Sub-attribute of:** flexibility

How should the product adjust as workload, data volume, or available resources change?

### Installability

**ID:** installability
**Sub-attribute of:** flexibility

What effort and operational disruption are acceptable when installing or removing the product?

### Replaceability

**ID:** replaceability
**Sub-attribute of:** flexibility

What must be preserved when this product takes the place of another product?

### Safety

**ID:** safety

Consider harm that product behavior could cause and the precautions needed to limit it.

### Operational constraint

**ID:** operational-constraint
**Sub-attribute of:** safety

Which operating limits must the product enforce to prevent hazardous behavior?

### Risk identification

**ID:** risk-identification
**Sub-attribute of:** safety

Which hazardous situations must the product recognize, and when must it recognize them?

### Fail safe

**ID:** fail-safe
**Sub-attribute of:** safety

What safe state should the product enter when continued operation cannot be trusted?

### Hazard warning

**ID:** hazard-warning
**Sub-attribute of:** safety

Which hazards require warnings, who must receive them, and how quickly?

### Safe integration

**ID:** safe-integration
**Sub-attribute of:** safety

What safety conditions must remain true when components are combined or connected?
