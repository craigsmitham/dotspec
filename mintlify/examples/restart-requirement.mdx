## Statement

After the service acknowledges a change, stopping and restarting it on the same
database must preserve each surviving entry’s identity, title and state. For a waiting entry, the expected reply and follow-up date must also survive. Deleted entries must stay absent.

## Applies to

**Targets:** [My checklist](<https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/specification/concept-uses/my-checklist.concept-use.spec-report.md>).

### Conditions

A local file on functioning storage, with each form submission acknowledged
before the process stops.

## Criterion

### Measure

Use the acknowledged changes to establish which entries should survive and in which view. Match each identity across all three views after restart. Count a discrepancy for each identity that is missing, appears unexpectedly or more than once, or has a different title, state or saved waiting detail. The cue for the saved date is assessed on the day of return under [Show which replies need follow-up](<https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/specification/requirements/show-which-replies-need-follow-up.requirement.spec-report.md>).

### Target

Zero discrepancies across Open, Waiting and Completed.

[Keep recorded work available](<https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/specification/requirements/keep-recorded-work-available.requirement.spec-report.md>).

## Evidence

The [restart acceptance test](<https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/tests/acceptance.py>) records entries in Open, Waiting and Completed through the forms, kills the service process without a clean shutdown and starts it again on the same database. It compares each view’s identities and titles with the acknowledged list. A missing, duplicated or unexpected entry fails the run, as would the return of either entry deleted before the stop. For both waiting entries it also compares the expected reply and follow-up date. The test does not stop the service during a change or simulate a power loss.

## Rationale

A list that loses track of earlier decisions forces the person to reconstruct them.
