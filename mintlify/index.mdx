---
title: "dotspec"
description: "Write software requirements in Markdown, check them with the CLI, and build a specification your team can read."
---

A dotspec project records what a software system must do and why. Its Markdown specifications can point to the exact model assertion, interface contract, or acceptance test that defines or checks a requirement.

For example, the To-do project promises that open tasks survive a restart. A reader can go from the requirement to its acceptance criteria and the test that checks them. [Read the restart requirement](https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/specs/requirements/restart.requirement.spec.md).

- [Build your first specification](/quickstart) to try the CLI in an empty directory.
- [Choose a handbook topic](/handbook) when you have a system, job, or requirement to describe.
- [Browse the generated To-do specification](https://github.com/dotspec-md/dotspec/blob/main/examples/todo/SPEC.md) to see how the pieces fit together.
