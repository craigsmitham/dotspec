# Timely recovery feedback

## Statement

The workspace must provide recovery feedback promptly enough for members to continue their task.

## Measurement plan

### Procedure

Measure elapsed time from submitting a recovery request to visible feedback.

### Sampling

Collect observations for representative recovery requests.

## Rationale

This deliberately incomplete draft supplies a measurement method but leaves the criterion, comparison rule and operating conditions to be established. Measurements alone will not establish conformance or intended-use fitness.
