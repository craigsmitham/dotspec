# Timely recovery feedback

## Statement

The workspace must provide recovery feedback promptly enough for members to continue their task.

## Criterion

### Measure

Elapsed time from submitting a recovery request to visible feedback.

## Verification plan

### Method

Measure the elapsed time for representative recovery requests.

## Rationale

This deliberately incomplete draft names a measure but leaves the required bound and operating conditions to be established. Measurements alone will not establish conformance or intended-use fitness.
