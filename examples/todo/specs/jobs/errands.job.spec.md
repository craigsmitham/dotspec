# Take care of an errand I have to leave for later

## Job

Take care of an errand that comes to mind before I can do it. The work starts when I decide it needs doing and ends when I know it is done or decide to let it go.

## Performer

The [list owner](../personal-task-list.system.spec.md#list-owner) fits personal errands around the rest of their day.

## Situation

At breakfast I remember the library books and a parcel I need to mail. I can get to the library this afternoon. Tomorrow, when I have time to go out again, how will I remember what is left?

## Supports

[Resume unfinished work](../personal-task-list.system.spec.md#resume-unfinished-work).

## Steps

### Work out what needs doing

Decide what would count as finished. Returning the books means taking every borrowed book back.

### Make sure it will come back to mind

Leave myself a way to remember when I can act. I might put the parcel by the door or jot a note beside my keys.

### Do what I can

Choose an errand that fits the opportunity and carry it out. Today I can return the books.

### Establish what is left

Check what actually happened. If I find a library book still in my bag, I still owe the library a visit.

## Outcomes

### Fewer missed opportunities

Reduce the chance of overlooking an unfinished errand when I have an opportunity to do it.

### Less time retracing my steps

Reduce the time it takes after a break to work out which errands still need attention.

## Evidence

This is a fictional occasion. Ask someone about the last errand they postponed. What brought it back to mind? How did they establish what was left? Someone who relies on seeing the parcel at the door may need a cue before they would think to consult a list.
