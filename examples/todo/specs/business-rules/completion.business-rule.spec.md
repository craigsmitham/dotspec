# Completion preserves a commitment

## Statement

A settled commitment must stay on the person's list until they delete it.

## Applies to

**Targets:** [My checklist](../personal-task-list.system.spec.md#my-checklist).

The rule applies whether the person did the last piece of work themselves or learned by reply that someone else had finished it.

## Authority

The recordkeeping policy chosen for this worked example. The person who keeps the list decides when to discard the record.

## Rationale

If the repair fails the next morning, the person can find yesterday's commitment and take it up again. Deleting the record gives up that option.
