# Completion preserves a commitment

[Personal task list](<../../SPEC.md>) · [Contents](<../index.md>)

## Statement

A finished commitment stays on the person's list until they explicitly delete it.

## Applies to

**Targets:** [Personal task list](<../../SPEC.md>).

## Authority

The product's personal recordkeeping policy.

## Rationale

A person can discover that a finished errand needs a follow-up.
