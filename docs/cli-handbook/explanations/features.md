# What holds a feature together?

What makes several behaviors one feature? Someone with a stake in the product can recognize what those behaviors let them do together. In To-do, **Pick up where I left off** lets me save an errand at breakfast and find it again tomorrow. Saving the parcel would do little good if it disappeared when I closed the list. Showing yesterday’s tasks would give me the wrong answer if the books I crossed off still appeared in Open. [Pick up where I left off](../../../examples/todo/specs/features/pick-up-where-i-left-off.feature.spec.md#groups).

That gives the feature a scope a reader can question. Suppose the team adds a count of open tasks to the same screen. A count of three helps someone judge their workload. They could still have no idea which errand they were working on. If selecting the count brought back their last unfinished errand, that behavior would help them resume it. Sharing a screen tells us where the change lives. Its contribution to the returning person’s task tells us whether it belongs in this feature.

IREB's glossary describes features in terms of stakeholder value and explains why one feature can span several requirements. Paschen and Fuchs give a fleet-management example where searching by vehicle category and searching by location belong together because they help someone find a vehicle. Their table also records the increment in which each ability appears. In dotspec, **Groups** records which specifications describe the benefit. The author can link to the same restart requirement from every feature that relies on it, so a change to the guarantee has one place to be made. [IREB, *Glossary of Requirements Engineering Terminology*, v2.2.0, “Feature”](https://cpre.ireb.org/en/downloads-and-resources/glossary?first-letter=F&tag=cpre-glossary) · [Paschen and Fuchs, “Bridging communication gaps with a Feature Tree” (2015), table 2](https://re-magazine.ireb.org/articles/feature-tree) · [To-do restart guarantee](../../../examples/todo/specs/requirements/restart.requirement.spec.md#statement).
